# Welcome to AvaLangStack

The AvaLangStack is a powerful suite of custom libraries designed to bring **Narrative Intelligence** and **Indigenous Relational Paradigms** to the forefront of LLM-powered application development. Building upon the robust foundation of LangChain.js, we create systems that are not only technologically advanced but also deeply accountable, context-aware, and aligned with a holistic understanding of intent and causality.

Explore our core packages and dive into how they transform LLM applications into relationally intelligent systems.
